# AI Math Notebook

A personal notebook for recreational, AI-assisted mathematical exploration.

This repository contains notes, experiments, computations, conjectures, failed approaches, and occasional candidate results arising from conversations with AI systems.

## Important disclaimer

Most entries in this repository are **exploratory research notes, not established mathematical results**.

They may contain:

- mistakes,
- incomplete arguments,
- unverified claims,
- rediscoveries of known results,
- incorrect citations or historical context,
- AI-generated arguments that have not been independently checked.

Unless an entry is explicitly marked otherwise, it should **not** be treated as a reliable mathematical reference.

I use AI extensively for exploration, proof attempts, computation, criticism, and rewriting. I do not claim personal credit for ideas merely because they were generated during my sessions.

The purpose of this repository is mainly to keep a public, searchable record of mathematical exploration.

## Status labels

Each note may contain one of the following labels.

- `exploratory` — early-stage exploration; likely incomplete or incorrect
- `candidate` — potentially interesting claim or argument, but not adequately verified
- `checked` — substantial internal checking has been performed, but no expert review
- `refuted` — the original claim or approach was found to be incorrect
- `inactive` — exploration paused without resolution
- `published` — developed into a publicly released paper or other formal write-up

These labels indicate my current level of confidence only.

## Topics

The repository may include work related to:

- Erdős problems
- integer sequences
- irrationality problems
- partition theory
- combinatorics
- Diophantine problems
- dynamical systems and Collatz-type problems
- experimental mathematics
- miscellaneous open problems

The choice of problems is driven mostly by curiosity.
